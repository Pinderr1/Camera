BedWatch

STARTING UP

Start once she is settled (in bed or on the couch). Alerts arm after 60 seconds.

1. Point the phone at the bed

Prop this phone up so the whole bed is in view, plugged in to power.

2. That's it for the camera

No zones needed — the app watches her posture (lying, sitting, standing) wherever she is in view. Move the phone to whichever room she's resting in: couch in the evening, bedroom at night.

2. Draw the bed zone

Tap each corner of the bed on the picture above (at least 3, usually 4).
