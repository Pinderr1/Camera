## Confirmed fixes

- Read the applied configuration snapshot in FLAME readiness/hooks instead of
  acquiring the site-editor lock under drone locks. Regressions cover API reads,
  site updates, fleet rebuilding, hook execution, live accessory edit/removal,
  and service startup/shutdown.
- Preserve malformed FLAME settings and their SHA-256 before a later save, and
  report degraded health instead of silently replacing the damaged evidence.
- Preserve unfinished FLAME form values, focus and selection across polling,
  while still refreshing monitoring status and the map.
- An FC failsafe interrupts an inspection even when the aircraft has already
  begun returning/landing, including emergency landings near home. The previous
  implementation could call that outcome completed. Physical aircraft remain
  uncommanded. Both a focused regression and SITL reproduced the defect before
  the fix and passed afterwards.
- Signing removal sends both an all-zero key and timestamp, while still signed
  by the current authorized pilot. ArduPilot otherwise installs a zero-valued
  key rather than disabling signing. Checked against the pinned firmware's
  [signing implementation](https://github.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/blob/Copter-4.7.2/libraries/GCS_MAVLink/GCS_Signing.cpp).
- Restart checks wait for confirmed reboot detection rather than treating a
  momentary link loss as a confirmed restart.
- Scenario restoration explicitly resets the disarmed flight mode, preventing
  inherited RTL/LAND from being reported as a new failsafe in the next test.
  Missing telemetry cannot be treated as proof of disarming.
- Stop after failed preparation/restoration; save incremental results with
  selected and not-run scenarios. A failed initial condition is reported with
  blockers. The disconnected-camera test establishes readiness before injecting
  its negative condition.
- Launchers own and stop only their SITL and drain processes, use unique log
  files, reconnect the SERIAL0 drain after reboot, and refuse occupied instances.
  The primary launcher delegates to this lifecycle rather than using `pkill`.
